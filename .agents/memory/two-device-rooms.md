---
name: Two-device rooms
description: The chosen multiplayer connection model for Hai Bộ Óc Một Ý.
---

Support two players on separate devices through an internet room code, while keeping same-device local play available.

**Why:** The user selected internet room codes for playing across two devices.

**How to apply:** Keep both online-room and local pass-and-play entry points available in future game changes.
